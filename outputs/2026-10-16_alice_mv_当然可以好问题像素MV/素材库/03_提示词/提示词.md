# 生图提示词与垫图对照

每条记录对应一张绿幕原稿：成品路径、垫图（按传入顺序，第 1 张在前）、画幅、完整提示词。路径都相对于 `素材库/`。
`prompts.json` 是同一份数据的机器可读版本。

## 通用做法

四种已验证的用法，后面的 62 条都属于其中一种。

| 类型 | 垫图 | 用途 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 新角色首张 | 角色设定图 + 画风参考图 | 从零生出第一张站姿 | `cardigan/stand`（alice_pixel_v1） |
| 同角色换动作 | 本角色站姿原稿（1 张） | 只改动作，其余保持 | `jk/*` 全部动作 |
| 同动作换衣服 | 图 1 是动作参考，图 2 是服装参考 | 把现成动作套到另一套衣服上，30 张 dress/chic/cardigan 都是这样出的 | `dress/bow` 等 |
| 场景图 | 角色原稿（1 张） | 带像素街景的朋友圈自拍，不要绿底 | `_备选与未采用/alice_selfie_dress_pixel` |

## 经验

- 背景写「flat solid green」，模型实际出的绿约为 #3FAE4A，不是写出来的 #00FF00。`pixel2svg.py` 按主色识别背景，不依赖精确色值。
- 画幅用 3:4，出图 864×1152。横躺的黄毛用 4:3。
- 同动作换衣服时，要在提示词里写「copy its pose, camera framing, canvas position and pixel-grid size exactly」，成品才能与 JK 套的网格间距一致（每格约 3.003 像素），换装时脚位和大小不会跳。
- 提示词里写「Image 1 is the POSE reference / Image 2 is the OUTFIT reference」，并保证传入顺序与之一致。
- 要求「接住」时，模型第一次画成了「请这边走」的引荐手势，保留为 `jk/present`，再用更明确的描述重画 `jk/catch`。
- 黄毛横躺欢呼画了 3 版：第 1 版脑后多出一只手，第 2 版仍有外来的托手，第 3 版采纳为 `huangmao/h_happy`。前两版在 `_备选与未采用/`。
- 从 JK 套换到其他衣服时，鞋、袜、饰品要逐项写清，三套服装描述见下表。

| 服装 | 提示词中的服装描述 |
|---|---|
| dress 碎花裙 | cream cable-knit cardigan over a pale floral dress, white socks, beige mary-jane shoes, gold star necklace |
| chic 白衬衫 | cream-white loose collared button-up shirt with gathered sleeves, light grey wide-leg trousers, white sneakers, gold star necklace |
| cardigan 开衫 | olive-green cable-knit cardigan over a white tee, beige cropped trousers, brown loafers, gold star necklace |

```text
Pixel art game avatar sprite of ONE chibi girl named Alice, full body, front view, standing, in the exact style of MapleStory character avatars (like the second reference image): crisp visible square pixels on a uniform pixel grid, about 100 pixels wide by 140 pixels tall in pixel resolution, upscaled with nearest-neighbor so each art pixel is a clean square block. Big head (head is about half of total body height), small body, short legs.

Character must match the first reference image (Alice chibi reference sheet) exactly:
- long natural wavy black hair falling past the shoulders, center part with soft bangs swept to both sides, hair rendered with several dark tones and soft cool grey highlights (MapleStory-style shaded hair strands)
- a smooth pale gold headband across the top of the head (always visible)
- very pale porcelain skin, large soft pink blush on both cheeks, tiny pink mouth with gentle smile
- big gentle warm brown eyes with dark upper lash line and two white highlights, MapleStory doll-like eyes, cute and kind expression
- small gold star pendant on a thin gold chain
- cream white crew-neck knit top, loose sage olive green cable-knit cardigan (#A39A6E) open at front with long sleeves
- beige khaki high-waist straight trousers, dark brown loafers
- no earrings, ears covered by hair, fully clothed modest outfit
- arms relaxed down at sides, small hands visible

Rendering: dark selective outline around the silhouette (darker shade of each local color, not pure black), soft pixel shading, clean and cute. Plain solid flat pure green background (#00FF00) filling the whole image, no shadow on the ground, no text, no name tag, no speech bubble, no other characters, no frame. Character centered with margin around it.
```

## 七日挑战面板底图 prompt 全文（逐字，成品见 `成品案例/七日挑战面板底图.png`）

```text
A warm, cheerful landscape illustration for a cozy casual-game event panel BACKGROUND, 4:3. NO TEXT ANYWHERE. This is a background plate: interactive reward cells will be overlaid later by the app, so the left zone must stay clean.

STYLE UNIFICATION IS THE TOP PRIORITY: one consistent clean 2D illustration style with soft cel shading, gentle rounded linework, light airy colors, bright cheerful daylight feeling, matching the style of the poster reference image. Polished cute mobile-game event screen feeling, NOT a flat corporate app UI.

OVERALL LAYOUT: the whole canvas is one big rounded game panel with a thick soft cream border and a subtle warm ivory parchment texture inside, floating on a soft peach-cream gradient background with tiny sparkles. A small decorative blank ribbon banner shape sits at the top center of the panel (no letters on it).

CRITICAL CLEAN ZONE: the LEFT 58 PERCENT of the panel interior must be a completely clean, smooth, even warm-ivory parchment area with nothing drawn on it — no slots, no frames, no props, no shadows, no sparkles. This entire zone is reserved for UI cells added later. Keep its texture very subtle and uniform so overlaid UI stays readable.

RIGHT SIDE — CHARACTERS: Alice and the fox mascot occupy the right 40 percent, standing fully inside the panel. FOX MASCOT must look exactly like the reference mascot: compact cute proportions, large round head, big glossy dark eyes, rosy cheeks, orange fur, white muzzle and white tail tip, mustard-yellow knit hoodie, striped scarf, dark blue trousers, brown boots; clearly a flat-cute mascot, not humanized, not furry, not 3D plush. Fox height about 60 percent of Alice. ALICE: young East Asian woman from the reference sheet — long softly wavy black hair, cream headband, warm brown eyes, delicate gold star necklace, open friendly smile; light cream knit cardigan over a muted cobalt top, warm-gray high-waisted trousers. Alice leans slightly toward the clean left zone with one open palm presenting it invitingly; the fox jumps cheerfully beside her holding one big shiny gold coin like the coin reference (gold coin with a rice-sprout emblem).

MINIMAL PROPS — RESTRAINT IS CRITICAL: decoration budget for the WHOLE image: exactly ONE small wrapped gift box with a ribbon sitting on the ground at the bottom-right corner near the fox's feet, plus at most TWO small gold rice-emblem coins leaning against that gift box on the ground, plus 3-4 tiny subtle sparkles near the characters. NOTHING ELSE. The entire UPPER HALF of the image must contain zero props, zero coins, zero gift boxes, zero leaves, zero flowers — only the clean panel, the blank ribbon banner, and Alice's head and raised hand. No floating coins anywhere (except the ONE coin in the fox's paw), no props in the top-right corner, no foliage in the corners.

PALETTE: warm cream and ivory base, cobalt-blue small accents, honey-gold coins, subtle peach and coral warmth. Absolutely no cold purple, no tech grid, no glossy black.

NO TEXT: absolutely no letters, no Chinese characters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark. Pure illustration only.
```

备注：概念稿 prompt 相同，仅把 CLEAN ZONE 段换成「EXACTLY SEVEN slots, top row FOUR + bottom row THREE, seventh larger with golden glow」段。

## 分享卡插画约束（踩坑沉淀，出处 PRD-share-card.md，成品见 `成品案例/共创插画_Alice与洛小山_分享卡底图.png`）

1. **画风统一是第一优先级**：狐狸保持头像那种扁平可爱造型（大圆头、大眼、腮红、芥末黄卫衣、条纹围巾），不能画成拟人化写实立绘，否则两个角色像来自两个世界
2. **必须有互动**：不能并排手插口袋平行站位（那是产品合影不是伙伴关系）；如 Alice 侧身微笑、手搭狐狸肩并向外伸手邀请，狐狸抬爪打招呼
3. **暖调**：奶油象牙底，蓝色只作品牌点缀；不要冷紫蓝、不要科技网格地面
4. **量化留白**：写百分比。只写「上 50% 留空」模型只给 37%，标题会压到角色头上
5. **插画内不含任何文字**：文字由前端 canvas 叠加，图里出现文字就没法复用
- 参考图 ≤ 3 张是 Alice 客户端的产品纪律；锁脸靠横版三视图设定表，提示词必须写「同一个人画了三次，不是三个人」
